The thesis

The SWP industry is the one axis you currently navigate to — eleven separate URLs. Make it the axis you navigate through: hold the district + region constant, and toggle the industry lens. The metadata strip stops being a label and becomes a live readout.

Below: the proposed toggle (live — click an industry), three icon treatments to choose from, and a radial alternate that leans into the atlas metaphor.

A · The industry rail live — click to toggle

Dimmed industries (e.g. Ag/Water, Education) are thin for this district — few member programs clear the BACCC priority bar. The toggle shows viability rather than presenting a 2-occupation sector as equal to a 30.

B · Icon treatments pick a direction

A · Ringed line

Geometric glyph inside a thin ring — echoes the Kallipolis sun/globe mark. Refined, institutional, sits quietly in a toolbar.

B · Solid form

Knockout glyph on a filled accent tile. Bold, high-recognition, reads at small sizes — strongest as a selected/active state.

C · Bare glyph

The platonic form alone, no frame. Maximal restraint — lets the accent color and silhouette carry the identity.
